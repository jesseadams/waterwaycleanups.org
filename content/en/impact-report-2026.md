## A Message from Our President

Our second year proved that this community is here to stay. In 2026 we held 16 cleanups, welcomed 301 volunteers, and removed 7,620 pounds of litter, up from 115 volunteers and 6,500 pounds in our first year.

This year brought some firsts. We partnered with the Aquia District of the National Capital Area Council to hold the first annual [Scouting for Clean Waterways](/projects/) event, where 152 Scouts and family members cleaned up Aquia Landing. Girl Scout Troop 15 and Cub Scout Pack 199 joined us for Earth Day, and we teamed up again with Friends of the Rappahannock for their Big Fall Cleanup. We also became a VDOT Adopt-a-Highway partner.

Our final cleanup of the year on Brooke Road and Thorny Point Road was our biggest single haul yet: 1,175 pounds of litter, including 20 tires.
